## Why "not a tech company"?

It's not about the tech. It's about the human on the other side.

We named ourselves "not a tech company" because the technology is just the tool. What matters is the problem we're solving — and the person we're solving it for.

### Philosophy

- **Empathy-driven design.** Every feature starts with a conversation, not a spreadsheet. We listen, we understand, and then we build what actually helps.
- **Real problems only.** No solutions looking for problems. We tackle the messy, real-life challenges that people actually face — like packing for a trip or capturing your kid's milestones.
- **Simple over clever.** We believe software should feel obvious. If you need a manual, we messed up. Our apps just work, the way you'd expect them to.

## Products

Tools designed around how you actually live — not how a product manager imagines you live.

### Pret — *Pack smart. Travel happy.* (Beta)

Your smart holiday packing assistant. Tell Pret where you're going, when, and with whom — and it takes the stress out of packing. No more forgotten swimsuits or three pairs of the same socks.

→ [Try the beta at pret-app.com](https://pret-app.com)

### Labeur — *Track your work. Simply.* (Live)

A simple work tracker that logs whether you worked today — and if you did, from home or the office. No accounts, no cloud sync, no tracking. Just your data, on your device. €0.99/month or €21.99 lifetime, iOS only.
