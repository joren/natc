Features

Everything you need. Nothing you don't.

Labeur does one thing well: it helps you keep track of your work days without getting in the way.

Quick Daily Logging

Open the app, tap once, done. Log your workday in under two seconds.

Home vs Office

Track where you worked — from home or at the office. See your patterns over time.

Privacy by Design

No analytics, no ads, no tracking pixels. We literally don't know you exist.

Bike Mileage Tracking

Automatically tracks your cycling distance to work — perfect for Belgian employees 🇧🇪.

Your data is yours alone.

Labeur was built with a simple philosophy: your work data is nobody's business but your own. We collect absolutely nothing. No analytics, no crash reporting, no usage data, no personal information. Everything lives on your device and never leaves it.
