## The Short Version

**Labeur does not collect, store, or transmit any of your personal data.** The app runs entirely on your device. We have no servers, no analytics, no tracking, and no way to access your information. That's it. That's the privacy policy.

If you want the detailed version for legal completeness, keep reading.

## 1. Who We Are

Labeur is developed and published by **Not A Tech Company**, a company registered in Belgium.

- Company: Not A Tech Company
- Location: Gent, Belgium
- VAT: BE1033.576.768
- Website: https://notatechcompany.be

## 2. Data We Collect

**None.** Labeur does not collect any data from you. Specifically:

- We do **not** collect personal information (name, email, phone number, etc.)
- We do **not** collect usage data or analytics
- We do **not** collect device identifiers
- We do **not** collect location data
- We do **not** use cookies or tracking pixels
- We do **not** use any third-party analytics or advertising SDKs
- We do **not** collect crash reports

## 3. Data Storage

All data you enter into Labeur (your work day logs, home/office preferences) is stored **exclusively on your device** using local on-device storage. This data:

- Never leaves your device
- Is not transmitted to any server
- Is not synced to any cloud service
- Is not accessible to us or any third party
- Is deleted when you delete the app

## 4. Third-Party Services

Labeur does **not** integrate with any third-party services. There are no analytics tools, no advertising networks, no social media SDKs, and no crash reporting services embedded in the app. The app makes **zero network requests**.

## 5. Children's Privacy

Since Labeur does not collect any data from anyone, it does not collect data from children. The app is safe for use by anyone regardless of age.

## 6. Data Sharing

We do not share any data with third parties because we do not have any data to share. There is no data to sell, transfer, or disclose.

## 7. Your Rights

Under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and other applicable privacy laws, you have rights regarding your personal data. Since Labeur does not collect or process any personal data, these rights are inherently fulfilled:

- **Right of access:** There is no data to access.
- **Right to erasure:** Deleting the app removes all locally stored data.
- **Right to data portability:** Your data exists only on your device and is under your full control.
- **Right to object:** There is no data processing to object to.

## 8. Changes to This Policy

If we ever change this privacy policy, we will update this page with the new policy and update the "Last updated" date at the top. Given our commitment to collecting zero data, we do not anticipate significant changes.

## 9. Contact

If you have any questions about this privacy policy, you can reach us through our website at https://notatechcompany.be.
