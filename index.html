Why "not a tech company"?

It's not about the tech. It's about the human on the other side.

We named ourselves "not a tech company" because the technology is just the tool. What matters is the problem we're solving — and the person we're solving it for.

Empathy-driven design

Every feature starts with a conversation, not a spreadsheet. We listen, we understand, and then we build what actually helps.

Real problems only

No solutions looking for problems. We tackle the messy, real-life challenges that people actually face — like packing for a trip or capturing your kid's milestones.

Simple over clever

We believe software should feel obvious. If you need a manual, we messed up. Our apps just work, the way you'd expect them to.
